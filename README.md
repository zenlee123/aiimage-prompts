# AI Image — Prompts & Creative Workflows

[AI Image](https://aiimage.com) is a browser-based workspace for generating images from text, editing existing pictures, and exploring new visual styles. This repository collects practical prompt templates and workflows for product visuals, social posts, illustrations, and presentations.

## Quick start

1. Open [AI Image](https://aiimage.com).
2. Choose an image model and the settings available for your task.
3. Copy a prompt below and customize the subject, palette, and composition.
4. Generate an image, review the result, and refine one detail at a time.

These are original prompt ideas, not a gallery of tested outputs. Results and available controls vary by model.

## A reusable prompt structure

**Subject + setting + composition + lighting + style + palette + constraints**

For example: A matte ceramic coffee cup on a pale oak table, close-up composition, soft morning window light, editorial product photography, warm cream and muted brown palette, uncluttered background, no text.

## Prompt collection

### 1. E-commerce product hero

> A minimalist reusable water bottle on a limestone pedestal, three-quarter view, soft studio lighting, subtle contact shadow, premium product photography, sage green and warm ivory palette, ample empty space on the right for a headline, no text or logos.

### 2. Landing page illustration

> An abstract workspace made from rounded geometric shapes, an open notebook, floating image frames and small creative tools, clean isometric illustration, pale blue and coral accents, light background, balanced composition with empty space on the left, no text.

### 3. Social media campaign

> A refreshing iced citrus drink on a sunlit outdoor table, bright summer editorial photography, vivid orange and turquoise, gentle condensation on the glass, playful shadows, centered subject with safe margins for a square crop, no lettering.

### 4. Presentation cover

> A flowing ribbon of translucent glass crossing a deep navy background, subtle blue and violet highlights, elegant cinematic lighting, wide composition, visual detail concentrated on the right, generous dark negative space on the left for a presentation title, no text.

### 5. Children's book illustration

> A small fox carrying a lantern through a peaceful woodland, hand-painted watercolor illustration, soft paper texture, warm amber light against muted teal foliage, gentle expression and a welcoming mood, no text.

### 6. Food photography

> A bowl of roasted vegetable pasta on a rustic wooden table, overhead composition, natural side lighting, realistic food photography, fresh herbs and warm earthy colors, simple linen napkin, appetizing detail, no text or logos.

### 7. Background replacement

Use with an uploaded product photo and an image editing mode.

> Keep the main product's shape, color, proportions and visible details. Replace the background with a clean warm-gray studio backdrop. Add a natural contact shadow and match the lighting to the product. Do not add objects or lettering.

### 8. Portrait restyling

Use with a reference photo you have permission to use.

> Restyle this portrait as a soft watercolor illustration. Keep the person's facial structure, expression and pose recognizable. Use a restrained pastel palette and a simple off-white background. Avoid adding accessories or changing the hairstyle.

### 9. Remove a distracting object

Use an editing mode that supports the requested change.

> Remove the distracting object in the upper-right background. Reconstruct that area using the surrounding texture and lighting. Keep the main subject, framing and all other visible objects unchanged.

### 10. Consistent visual series

> Create a product scene featuring [PRODUCT] on a pale stone surface. Use the same visual direction throughout the series: soft light from the upper left, muted sage and ivory colors, eye-level camera, minimal props and generous negative space. For this variation, include [ONE DISTINCTIVE PROP]. No text.

## Practical workflows

### Create a product campaign

- Start with a clear reference image where supported.
- Define a fixed palette, lighting direction and composition.
- Change only the setting or prop between variations.
- Inspect product details before publishing.
- Select the aspect ratio for each intended channel.

### Improve a first draft

| Problem | Prompt adjustment |
| --- | --- |
| Too busy | Ask for fewer props and more negative space. |
| Wrong mood | Specify lighting, palette and atmosphere. |
| Subject too small | Request a closer crop or a larger subject. |
| Inconsistent edits | Explicitly state what should remain unchanged. |
| Unreliable lettering | Generate artwork with empty space, then add final copy in a design editor. |
